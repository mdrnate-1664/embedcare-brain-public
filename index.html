The EmbedCare Brain

EmbedCare is a white-label telehealth platform. In about six weeks its president, Nate, built a system where every conversation the company has lands in one private, versioned store, and scheduled AI routines read that store and do the day's operating work. This page shows what runs, when, and what each piece leaves behind for the next one. Nothing reaches a client without a human tap.

The day clock

When each scheduled job fires, on a 24-hour strip. Circles run every day or every weekday. Squares run weekly. Hover for the name, click for the card. Webhooks, on-demand tools and the always-on loops are not plotted; they run all day. On a narrow screen, scroll the strip sideways for the afternoon.
